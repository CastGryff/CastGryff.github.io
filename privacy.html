Homebridge Nest privacy policy

Homebridge Nest uses Google Device Access to read the Nest thermostat selected by the owner and send thermostat commands. It uses the device’s name, room, temperature, humidity, operating mode, setpoints, and update events to display the thermostat and its state in Apple Home.

Google Cloud Pub/Sub delivers update events. The owner’s Homebridge installation stores the Google connection credentials and may log device status or errors during operation. Thermostat data and controls are available to people or services the owner has authorized to use their Homebridge or Apple Home. This site does not receive thermostat data. The integration does not sell the data or use it for advertising.

The owner can manage or revoke the Nest connection and remove the integration and its credentials from Homebridge.
